How it works

From recording to quiz, with no manual work

1Recording

Your Google Meet lesson is recorded and saved to your Google Drive.

2Automatic pick-up

Every hour, the system checks the folder and collects any new recordings.

3Transcription

The audio is turned into text by Whisper on Groq, in English and Portuguese.

4Quiz generation

AI writes the questions from the transcript and the lesson's PowerPoint, at the student's level.

5Your review

The quiz is saved as a draft and you're notified to check it.

6Publishing

You tweak anything you like and publish. Nothing reaches a student without your approval.
