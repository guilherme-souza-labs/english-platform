Your 15 questions

Answers to your brief

01Which first release do you recommend, and what would you defer?+

The public website is the first delivery on every plan. I'd recommend the Complete plan: R$ 15,900 list price, or R$ 11,900 if agreed in November. I'd leave self-booking and AI practice for later.

02Will you handle design and development? Who's responsible for copy, translation, images, testing and maintenance?+

I handle design, development, testing and maintenance. The Portuguese version is translated automatically by AI and you review it. You provide the draft English text and photos, and sign off the final versions.

03Which platform would you use, and why?+

A bespoke system in React, with Supabase Cloud as the database and hosting on a Hostinger VPS or AWS, all in your name. There's no per-student charge or website-builder licence, and everything can be moved to another server. Builders such as Base44 struggle with specific rules like replacement credits and make it harder to change supplier.

04Can you show me how I'd edit a package, update availability and upload a lesson?+

Yes. At sign-off you'll do all three yourself. If needed, I'll record video tutorials on request and keep them in a Google Drive folder. Only new pages and layout changes need me.

05What's the full price, and the ongoing cost?+

Essential R$ 4,500, Professional R$ 8,400 and Complete R$ 15,900 (R$ 11,900 if agreed in November), or each item individually at the prices in the table. Automated WhatsApp and lesson conversion are add-ons. Running costs are set out above and paid by you directly to each provider. All prices in Brazilian reais.

06How will English and Portuguese be handled?+

Every page exists in both languages, with a language switch throughout. The Portuguese version is translated automatically by AI and reviewed by you. In the editor, the two texts sit side by side. Forms, emails and the portal follow each student's language.

07How will recurring slots, time zones, daylight saving and rescheduling work?+

Weekly lessons are anchored to São Paulo time, and the system works out each student's local time, including daylight-saving changes abroad. Rescheduling a lesson changes only that date. Students propose times and you confirm them.

08Can you demonstrate replacement credits, individual rates and renewals without losing lessons?+

Yes. Every purchase, replacement and lesson delivered is recorded with a reason, and the balance is always calculated, never typed in. The 8 new lessons plus 2 replacements case is a sign-off test for Phase 3.

09How will private files and student records be protected?+

Each student can only access their own information, files open through links that expire, your login has two-factor authentication, and there's a daily backup. You can remove my access at any time.

10What needs checking before a fixed price, and what happens if an integration fails?+

I'd need to confirm your Mercado Pago, Stripe and Google accounts (and Twilio, if you take the add-on), your accountant's process, and which WhatsApp messages you'd like automated. If an integration fails, you can record the payment or lesson manually and carry on.

11How will PowerPoint files be handled?+

Students can download the original or read the PDF you export. Clickable answer boxes only work in PowerPoint; for interactive exercises in the browser, there are the online quizzes and the lesson conversion add-on.

12What will I review at each milestone, and how are changes handled?+

In each phase you review the design and then a working test version, via a link. That's 2 rounds on the design and 1 on the test version. Anything out of scope is quoted in writing first.

13What testing will you do before delivery?+

iOS and Android phones, accessibility, forms reaching the right place, test students trying to access each other's data, time zones, and payments in test mode. Any defects during the support period are fixed free of charge.

14Who owns what?+

It's all yours: domain, content, design, code and accounts. The code lives in a repository in your name and the database can be exported in full.

15What training, documentation and support are included?+

Video tutorials on request, kept in a Google Drive folder, technical documentation for any future developer, and the support period of your chosen plan.
